What is WordCamp?

A conference built by the community, for the community.

WordCamps are informal, community-organized events covering everything WordPress — from your first "Hello World" post to core contribution, security, SEO, and scaling a web business. No corporate stage-buying, no gatekeeping — just people who build the open web, together.

And Delhi isn't just another stop on the map. India's very first WordCamp happened right here in February 2009, with WordPress co-founder Matt Mullenweg in the room. Everything that followed — hundreds of meetups, WordCamp Asia coming to India, WordCamp India's flagship status — traces back to this city.

Contributor Day
