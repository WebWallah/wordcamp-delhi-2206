about_wcdel

A conference built by the community, for the community.

WordCamps are informal, community-organized events covering everything WordPress — from your first "Hello World" post to core contribution, security, SEO, and scaling a web business. No corporate stage-buying, no gatekeeping — just people who build the open web, together.

WordCamp Delhi has become one of India's most active WordPress communities, bringing together developers, designers, agency folks, and the curious from across the country and beyond.

who_its_for

why_this_year

WordPress powers 40%+ of the web and is quietly becoming core infrastructure for the agentic web. Delhi's thriving developer community is ready for the next chapter.

what_to_expect

Two days. One community. Endless chai refills.

A snapshot of the format past Delhi editions have run — the 2026 schedule builds on it.

Day 0

Contributor Day

Hands-on contribution to WordPress core, docs, support, translation, marketing, and training — mentors included, laptop required, experience optional.

Multi-track

Talks & Workshops

Beginner, developer, and business tracks running in parallel — from your first site to Gutenberg blocks to scaling an agency.

Expo Hall

Sponsor Showcase

Meet the hosts, plugin makers, and agencies behind the products you use daily — swag included, no sponsor speaking slots (that's the rule).

Hallway Track

Networking Over Food

The best sessions happen between sessions — over chaat, chai, and the kind of conversation a Zoom call can't replicate.

All Welcome

First-Timer Friendly

Never been to a WordCamp? Half the room hasn't either. Beginner track and volunteer buddies make sure nobody's lost.

After Hours

The After Party

Because the community doesn't clock out at 6 PM. Music, food, and the informal reunion of the day.
