01 / why attend

Built for people who ship.

Not a trade show. A working conference for developers, designers, agency owners, and the WordPress-curious.

Deep technical content

Core internals, Gutenberg block development, performance, security — talks written for people who already know the basics.

A full Contributor Day

Ship a patch, fix a bug, write docs, or mentor someone doing it for the first time — a full day, mentors included.

An honest community

Independently organized by volunteers. No pay-to-speak sponsor slots, no vendor keynotes disguised as talks.
